🗑️ User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 2026

Meta Platform Compliance

Raj FB Pro is a private automation workflow. We do not store or retain any personal information of Facebook users.

If you wish to remove Raj FB Pro access permissions from your Facebook Account, follow these standard Facebook steps:

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy ➔ Settings.
  2. Scroll down to Security and select Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Raj FB Pro in the list of authorized applications.
  4. Click the Remove button.
  5. Confirm removal. This immediately revokes all access tokens and disconnects your account from the automation suite.

Any temporary telemetry cached in GitHub Pages will be automatically purged during the next 2-hourly sync routine.
